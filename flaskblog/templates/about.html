About AbyNest

A simple space to write, share, and revisit ideas.

AbyNest is a lightweight blogging app built for personal posts, quick updates, and clean reading. It keeps the focus on the writer, the post, and the people behind each story.

What You Can Do

  • Create an account and manage your profile.
  • Publish posts with a title and full content.
  • Edit or delete your own posts when needed.
  • Browse posts by author and date.

Built With

  • Flask for the web application.
  • PostgreSQL for storing users and posts.
  • Flask-Login for account sessions.

This project is also a practical learning app: it brings together routing, templates, authentication, image uploads, email reset flow, and database work in one complete Flask project.
